# The House Below — full walkthrough

Spoilers follow. J gives a shorter contextual lead in game. H outlines interactive objects.
Exit buttons travel in any verb mode; right-click cancels an inventory selection.
Conversations begin with general questions. Examine clues and listen to answers to unlock
specific follow-ups. Each menu shows three questions; an asked question disappears and the next
one takes its place, and new questions appear as you learn things.

1. Begin in the **back seat of the motorcar**, talking to the coachman. Optional questions
   introduce Arthur Pike; Leave ends the ride, and Escape skips to arrival. Outside,
   he waits beside the car on the left and encourages you to get out of the rain.
   Click **FRONT DOOR > Knock / enter**, or ACT on the lit door. James greets you there.
   Choose **Show Uncle Frank's invitation**, or select the inventory letter before knocking.
   The scene fades into the entrance hall. **Go outside** returns to the car and coachman;
   questions already asked in the car do not come back.
2. Visit the library and take the brass clock hand from the book. In the guest room, use it
   on the tall grandfather clock. Set **8:20**. The hidden stair exit becomes available.
3. Take the hairpin from the dining table. In the study, use it on the locked desk. Keep the
   evidence ledger. Return to Elias in the library and discuss the ledger to receive the ward key.
4. Look at the chapel glass: **moon, star, sun**. Use that sequence on the study safe and take
   the generator fuse. Take the oil can from the conservatory.
5. Descend the clock stair to the generator. Use the fuse on the cabinet and the oil on the
   motor bearings. The ventilation fan starts once both are fitted.
6. In the lower ward, speak to Clara and Thomas. In the archive, speak to Miriam and inspect
   the nameplates. Set the register to **Elias, Clara, Thomas, Miriam**.
7. At the boiler controls choose **ISOLATE**, then **RETURN**, then **RELEASE**. Wrong orders
   reset safely. Power must already be restored.
8. Read the attic laboratory notebook. Mix **azure, amber, clear** in the apparatus. Use the
   reversal vial on the lower ward's vapor inlet after the dosing line has been isolated.
9. Reach the boathouse through the conservatory and take the rope. Return via the house and
   clock stair to the reservoir. Use the rope on the bridge anchor.
10. Play **C–E–G** on the chapel organ to obtain the tuning fork. Use it on the reservoir's
    resonant latch after securing the rope. The crossing is now safe in both directions.
11. With names restored, air cleared, and crossing safe, use Elias' key on the ward gate.
    Clara, Thomas, Miriam and Elias gather at the boathouse.
12. Click the rowboat. Choose to bring the evidence to the village or entrust the sealed ledger
    to the survivors. Both endings save the four patients. Completed progress is stored in slot 3.

No unique item is consumed by a wrong guess. All clues remain available. You can revisit rooms
after the ending or restore an earlier slot. F5 and F9 open the three-slot save/load selectors.
